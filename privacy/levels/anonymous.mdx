description: "The frontier rung. Your identity is detached from the request before the provider sees it."
---

Anonymous is the first rung of the [privacy ladder](/privacy/levels) and the level frontier models run at. The provider gets your request from Nito, without your identity attached. Reach for it when you want a frontier model and the call does not need retention switched off upstream or a proof of where it ran.

## What Anonymous Is

Nito sits between you and the model provider. Your account and your identity stop at Nito, and the provider receives the request from Nito and answers it. The provider sees the content of the request. It does not see who sent it.

That is the whole guarantee, and it is the most that can be arranged for a model only its own provider can serve. A frontier model runs on its provider's own infrastructure, so there is no way to switch retention off on their side or to seal the execution in hardware. Detaching your identity is what remains available, and it is worth having.

## What Anonymous Guarantees

- **Your identity is not passed upstream.** The request reaches the provider from Nito.
- **Nito keeps neither your prompt nor the response.** Prompts, completions, reasoning, tool payloads, and attachment contents are not persisted. Where a chat history exists, it is on your machine.

What Anonymous does not carry is a commitment from the provider about what it does with the request after answering, or any attestation. If you need retention switched off upstream, use [Private](/privacy/levels/private). If you need proof of the environment that ran the call, use [Confidential](/privacy/levels/confidential).

## How to Select Anonymous

You select Anonymous by picking a model whose level is Anonymous. There is no privacy flag on your command: the level follows the model. Browse the catalog with the `models` command to see each model's level, then set the one you want with `model` or `--model`.

Frontier models sit here. Open-weight models start at [Private](/privacy/levels/private) and climb from there, so picking an open-weight model lands you above Anonymous rather than on it. The [Model List](/models/all-models) groups every model by level.

## Trade-offs

- **No upstream retention promise.** The provider is not held to zero data retention at this level. That is the line between Anonymous and Private.
- **No proof to check.** If the value of a call depends on verifying where it ran, Anonymous cannot give you that. Pick a Confidential model for an attestation.
- **The frontier models are here.** The models you cannot get any other way sit at this level, which is the reason to use it.

## When to Choose Anonymous

Reach for Anonymous when you want a specific frontier model and the request is ordinary work: prototyping, public or non-confidential prompts, general coding questions, anything where detaching your identity is enough. When the content itself is sensitive, move to a model that carries a stronger level and let the level travel with it.
